Your interviews and your chosen services

Privacy policy

This policy covers IdeaForge for the web and Android. Interviews stay on your device, while the content needed for model requests and optional voice services goes to the services you choose. There is no IdeaForge account or sync server.

Developer and privacy contact

IdeaForge is maintained as the open-source jaypetez/ideaforge project. This policy describes the app's data handling, not the independent policies of your model provider, speech service, browser or hosting service.

Privacy contact: jayson@duck.com

Data accessed and kept on the device

IdeaForge uses the idea, questions, answers, unfinished drafts, extracted facts, coverage, generated write-up, names and tags to conduct, resume, search and export interviews. Whatever you put in an idea can contain personal information; the app does not separate that information from the rest of your text.

What goes to a model provider, and when

Generating a follow-up question or write-up sends interview context to the selected provider: the opening idea, questions and answers (compacted as needed), facts and coverage. The provider returns generated questions, suggestions or a write-up, which become part of the local interview. Connection/key checks and model-list reads also contact the selected service, without sending an interview.

Hosted inference choices are Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq and OpenRouter. The relevant API key is sent to that provider to authenticate the request. OpenRouter also routes inputs to an underlying model provider; review its provider data practices. Local choices are Ollama (local), LM Studio (local) and Another local server. In the web-only Claude (this viewer) path, the hosting Claude viewer receives the prompt through its sampling capability; no IdeaForge API key is needed.

IdeaForge does not proxy these requests through an application server. Provider logging, retention, training, abuse monitoring and account controls are governed by the selected service and your account settings, not by deleting an interview in IdeaForge. A local address is not a promise about what that server does after receiving your content.

Microphone, transcription and spoken replies

Voice input requires microphone permission. Browser recognition and Android's speech recognition service process your audio, on-device where available or through the service's own remote processing. Choosing a device voice or recognizer is not an offline guarantee.

The web record-and-transcribe path uploads recorded audio to the selected Groq Whisper or OpenAI transcription service, with its transcription key, language and a text hint where used. A hint can contain the current question and earlier transcribed words. On Android, this hosted fallback runs only when native recognition is unavailable or cannot handle the language, and only with its own key and explicit audio-transfer consent. The returned words are stored as interview text, not as audio.

Native/device speech is the default. Optional OpenAI speech sends the text being read aloud to OpenAI, using a separate speech key. That text can contain your idea, questions, examples and refined prompt. Hosted speech requires consent to text transfer, an AI-generated voice and metered usage, plus a successful hosted check/preview. The preview itself sends text to OpenAI. A failed hosted reply can visibly fall back to the device voice; cancellation does not start fallback playback.

In Android hands-free mode, a microphone foreground service keeps the active voice interview listening when the screen is off, with notification controls to pause or exit. Pause cancels the capture and pending transcription, keeping the available native caption and completed transcribed segments as a draft. An unfinished hosted-transcription segment is not added after pausing. Exit preserves captured words.

On the web, Pause releases owned microphone/playback resources, but an already captured recording may finish transcription to preserve a draft. Exit keeps transcribed words and cancels unfinished audio. Neither platform can recall data already sent to a provider. The app does not run a background wake-word listener.

Secure handling and network limits

Hosted model, transcription and speech connections use HTTPS. The web app permits user-entered model endpoints only on loopback. Android also permits private-network model servers. A plain http:// connection is not encrypted in transit: another machine on your LAN can receive interview text, and network observers may be able to read it. Use HTTPS on that server when available; a local model does not make hosted voice services local.

Credential encryption does not encrypt your interviews or exports. It does not protect an unlocked device, a compromised operating system/browser or a malicious script already running as the web app's origin. Keep keys scoped, protect your device and treat interview files as sensitive. See Privacy and security for the browser's CSP, storage and encryption boundaries.

IdeaForge contains no analytics, advertising, telemetry, user accounts or application sync service, and does not sell interview data. Hosting and provider services still receive ordinary request metadata, such as an IP address and request headers. The web app also requests its IBM Plex stylesheet and font files from Google Fonts, without interview content or an IdeaForge key in those asset requests. This guide uses local assets and system fonts.

Retention, deletion and your copies

Local interviews have no automatic expiry. They remain until you delete an idea or clear the app's storage; browser storage can also be evicted. Use the library's Delete action for an idea. Browser site-data controls remove that origin's stored data and caches; clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes its local app data. Back up first if you need it. There is no IdeaForge server copy from which to restore a lost interview.

The web app’s Settings → Your data → Forget keys and Android’s confirmed Forget action remove the saved inference, transcription and speech credentials. They do not delete interviews or revoke credentials at their providers. Revoke a compromised key through the provider's account controls. Non-secret preferences generally remain; permissions tied to hosted speech/transcription are withdrawn.

Android creates a temporary private export file when sharing; older share files are pruned on a later app start or share. A destination app, clipboard, downloaded Markdown or JSON backup is a separate copy outside IdeaForge's control. Delete or secure those copies separately. Deleting locally cannot erase provider-side records or copies already shared. Ask the relevant provider about its retention/deletion options; there is no IdeaForge account to delete.

Adult audience

IdeaForge is intended for people aged 18 or over, not for children. The app does not collect an age field or verify your age.

Changes and questions

Changes to data handling will be reflected on this page and in relevant in-app disclosures. Review the policy when updating the app or changing services. Send privacy questions to the contact address above; do not include a live API key or sensitive transcript in a public issue. The repository records policy changes alongside their implementation.
